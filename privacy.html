Privacy Policy for Suraj (Suraj English Academy) Last Updated: June 18, 2026 Suraj English Academy, Palanpur ("we," "our," or "Academy") operates the automated WhatsApp chatbot assistant Suraj (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our students and users ("you" or "your"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you interact with our App. By messaging the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. 1. Information We Collect When you interact with the App, we collect the following types of information: WhatsApp Profile Information: Your WhatsApp phone number and your public WhatsApp display name. Message Content: The text messages, English grammar questions, and greetings you type and send directly to the App. Language Preference: The language you use to communicate, which is automatically processed to respond to you in the same language. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected data strictly to run and improve the grammar educational experience: To Answer Grammar Doubts: Your text input is processed to identify English grammar queries and generate appropriate educational responses. To Personalize Interaction: We use your greeting language to match our output language for your ease of understanding. To Filter Off-Topic Messages: Automated logic scans your input; if the message is unrelated to English grammar or greetings, it is strictly filtered to a "No" response to maintain academic focus. 3. Data Processing and Third-Party Services To provide real-time automated AI responses securely, your messages flow through approved, high-standard infrastructure. We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed through: Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp Business): Your messages are securely transmitted through Meta's official infrastructure. Google Gemini API: Valid grammar queries are securely processed via Google Gemini API's developer framework to generate educational explanations. Workflow Automation (n8n): Data is processed through our self-hosted automation workflows to connect the API services. 4. Data Retention We do not permanently log or store your personal chat history for commercial tracking. Messages are processed in real-time within the 24-hour customer service window allowed by Meta. Anonymized grammar questions may be reviewed internally by Suraj English Academy solely to understand common student doubts and improve our curriculum. 5. Children's Privacy Our App is an educational tool meant to solve English grammar doubts. If a student under the age of 13 uses the tool, we only process the text inputs to return grammar answers. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's digital and educational interactions. 6. Security of Your Data We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your phone number and message contents from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our automated nodes utilize secure, private API keys to communicate between Meta and Google servers. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We advise you to review this policy periodically for any changes. 8. Contact Us If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: Suraj English Academy Palanpur, Gujarat, India
